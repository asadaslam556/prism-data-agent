# Code of Conduct

Prism is a small project, and I'd like it to stay a place where anyone can ask a question, report a bug or send a pull request without having a bad time.

## What's expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including toward people who are new to this.
- Keep feedback about the code, not the person writing it.
- Accept that people disagree, and that "no" or "not right now" on a feature is a normal answer.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwanted attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing issues and discussions.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you're representing the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact me privately through [asadaslam.tech](https://asadaslam.tech/). Reports are kept confidential. I may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who don't follow this code.

Asad Aslam, maintainer
